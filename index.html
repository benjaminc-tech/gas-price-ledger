    Verified on-chain. Reading the contract...

    Contract . Nobody, including its owner, can edit a price once written; corrections become new revisions.

    How this works
    • Every week the owner's wallet fetches the EIA's new prices and writes them to the contract. Writing costs a small fee ("gas") in test ETH.
    • Reading is free and open to anyone. This page sends raw eth_call requests to a public Sepolia node and decodes the answers itself: no server, no database, no libraries.
    • It runs on Ethereum's Sepolia test network, so the coins have no value. The technology is the same as the real thing.
    Data: U.S. Energy Information Administration, regular gasoline, weekly.
